FAQ

What materials do you use for my creations?

Pieces of fallen wood or from pruning trees.
I cut by using a little axe each piece in smaller parts and waiting months before it is ready fir carving. There are also some kind of particular wood that I would like to work, but such trees are not growing in my region, so I’m purchasing it from a wooden store or from online sellers .

How do you realize your hand carved jewelry?

Preparing a design to copy on wood, or drawing it directly on wood by my fantasy. After cutting the part of wood out the design, I prepare my sharpen tools , in most of cases knives and some gauges, choosing a righ size, thickness of the blade or the knife’s tip, for a specific part of carving. At finished work, I apply a natural oil (tung oil) finishing, at least 3 times, once per day, enough to protect wood and giving it a beautiful final effect.

Can I ask you to make a personalized custom order ?

I can realize a personalized work, that can be jewelry or something different, just enough big for smaller tools that I use. After you tell me your request, I can advise what is possible to do, and also helping you to take a right decision if you may not be sure at all what you would like.

What are details if I ask for a personalized ring?

Making my rings is a more particular work than other jewelry. Even a simpler ring takes more time to do than a simple pendant. I carve simple style of rings like just the band where I can engrave some word , phrases or also carving a low relief design, and the band can be of different shape, as you prefer. You can also ask me for a very particular ring with a design out the band, that could be a symbol, flowers, leaves, animals, and more, and it does depend how detailed you would like the design.
The ring’s wide depends on your preferences, but also the design, like for example less than 1cm to over 2cm large, if needs more space to work something very detailed.
The thickness is a little bit more than metal rings, although I prefer use a harder wood, especially because I’m not using some chemical finishing products to make it be stronger, but just applying natural oil finishing. My particular rings suit mostly to wearing for some special occasion, instead of all day and night. You can ask me for any personalized size. Just in some cases if the design would be difficult, I need to value if it could be carved on a smaller size.

How do I take care about my wood artisan items?

Although the oil protection from humidity, could be good to apply sometimes an organic oil for wood, and after a few minutes dry it with a cloth if the piece is simpler or using a soft brush (like toothbrush for little pieces), to remove oil into details pieces. That could happen if you would like to make it look shine again, that wood can lose a little this effect in time, but even faster because of water. So my advice is avoid long contact with water, like swimming or bathing.

Do you offer a special package for gift?

My creations are shipped already in nice pouches or boxes with design of my logo. If you specify me in notes that your order is for a gift, I can use my boxes, or you can ask me for something personalized even adding a card , texting a note with wishes.
