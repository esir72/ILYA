About me

Craft passed down through the years, and a love for nature in every piece.

My name is Ilya. I have been carving wood for over 20 years. For me, wood is a living material that guides its own form.

Each piece is handmade, from selecting the blank to the final sanding. I use only natural oils and waxes for finishing to preserve the wood's texture and warmth.

I work with all types of wood. I am happy to discuss custom orders tailored to your wishes.
